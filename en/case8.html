General description of the project

Educational project at the British Higher School of Art & Design - the concept of a touch remote control for the SberDevices and SberBox ecosystem

General description

As part of the British School of Design program, we worked on a training brief from SberDevices: to improve the SberBox control experience and offer our own view of the remote control - with an emphasis on innovation, non-trivial input formats and honest ergonomics. The project combined product research, UX/UI of the TV interface and interaction scenarios with voice assistants of the Salyut family.

As a team, we went through an analysis of existing materials and interfaces, a series of in-depth interviews and quantitative surveys, many iterations of the shape and layout of buttons, and brought it to a clear concept with a display on the body, touch modes, a gyroscope and onboarding. Important limitations remained the line of visual style of SberDevices, the presence of a microphone and the cost guideline - as in a real product task.

The result is a complete story about the touch remote: from audience research and pain points of the current device to highly detailed remote screen layouts, TV settings and user scenarios. This is a demonstration of how design education interfaces with an industrial partner and an engineering framework, not just a visual presentation.

My role in the project

Quantitative survey, in-depth interviews, interface prototyping, presentation of the project to a wide audience

Conducting a quantitative survey +

Collected and analyzed responses from respondents about the current remote control and expectations for the new one: scenarios for using SberBox, preferences for shape, buttons and digital functions. The survey results helped the team choose directions for prototypes and weed out weak hypotheses early on.

Conducting in-depth interviews +

Conducted semi-formal interviews with set-top box users: how they search for content, use voice, a mobile remote control application, and a physical remote control. Pains were identified from speech insights (for example, recognition of the assistant button, hierarchy on TV), which then formed the basis for scripts and layouts.

Interface prototyping +

I collected iterations of the remote control and interface screens on TV: application grids, touchpad and cursor modes, onboarding, profile settings. We quickly tested ideas on low-level mockups and brought them to a visually cohesive look within the SberDevices UI-kit.

Presentation of the project to a large audience +

Prepared and presented a team project in front of the school curators, partner representatives and a large audience at the defense: story structure, slides and demonstration of key scenarios of the touch panel from research to the final concept.
