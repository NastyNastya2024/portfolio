Project Description

GEO / SEO landing page and approach to promoting a noticeable brand

General description

The project included the development and publication of a GEO/SEO platform landing page: a one-page website with a clear structure (key offer, blocks about promotion sites and client industries, FAQ), adaptive layout and placement on GitHub Pages. The resource shows how the service brings brands organically and maintains a unified image in the digital environment.

For promotion, the GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) approach is used in conjunction with classic SEO. We are talking not only about positions in search results by keywords, but about ensuring that the brand consistently appears where people are looking for it: in search engines and on maps, in catalogs and reviews, as well as in the responses of AI assistants and LLMs. The content and structure of the site lays out relevant entities, facts and query scenarios; Local and reputational signals are built in parallel to increase visibility, CTR and trust.

The result is a combination of “product on site” and “GEO method”: the platform explains the service, and the methodology describes how this service gives the brand visibility “everywhere” the user searches or asks.

Website

The layout and implementation of the page, visual presentation and typography, blocks about channels and industries, a contact form and legal links were created. The site is available for viewing via the link above; a preview is built into the case for a quick UX assessment.

GEO/AEO promotion

Emphasis on organics in search and on maps, on local and vertical scenarios, on the presence of the brand in search results with generation and in voice interfaces. The goal is to make the brand not just found, but the preferred source of response and choice.
