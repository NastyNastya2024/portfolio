Design, branding and development

Three directions: design, branding, development

Design

  • UX/UI of a mobile marketplace for AI creators: ordering services and content, feed, work between customers and creators

  • Ready— key screens are reflected in the preview above

Branding

  • Product rebranding: visual concept, typography and palette

Development
