General description

The case is implemented on the Twin platform, of which I am a partner. The platform is a designer of voice and chat bots, as well as a tool for automated calls and lead generation, which allows you to quickly launch bots for specific business tasks.

As part of the case, an NPS survey was developed and launched for a SPA center in order to collect feedback, analyze customer satisfaction and improve the quality of service. Additionally, the platform allows you to implement lead generation calls, appointment reminders, feedback collection and marketing campaigns to increase repeat sales and customer loyalty.

About the project

Voice NPS survey and calls for a SPA center on the Twin platform.

What platform is used in the case? +

The Twin platform is a voice and chat bot builder, as well as a tool for automated calls and lead generation.

What is my role in the project? +

I am a partner of the Twin platform and independently designed, configured and launched the bot for the business tasks of the SPA center.

What problem was being solved? +

Collecting customer feedback and measuring NPS after a visit to the SPA center, as well as improving the quality of service.

What survey format was used? +

Automated voice polling using phone calls.

What data was collected? +

NPS score, customer comments, call rate and survey completion rates.

What did the introduction of a bot do for business? +

Regular feedback, reduction of manual workload on staff and transparent metrics of customer satisfaction.

Can the solution be scaled? +

Yes, the scripts can be easily adapted to other organizations in the beauty industry or network without modifying the code.

What additional scenarios are possible? +

Lead generation, registration for services, return of “sleeping” clients, reminders of visits and information about promotions.

Is the solution suitable for small businesses? +

Yes, thanks to the low-code approach and quick launch without complex development.
